# CampusMarket Google Play Preparation

## Store listing

**App name:** CampusMarket

**Short description:** Buy, sell and discover useful student items on your campus.

**Full description:**

CampusMarket is a student-focused marketplace for discovering affordable items, publishing personal listings and communicating directly with sellers. Browse a product catalogue, search by title, filter by category, save favourites and manage active or draft listings. Continue viewing saved products when connectivity is interrupted and synchronise supported Firebase changes after reconnection. CampusMarket supports English and Tshivenda, Firebase authentication, Google Sign-In and real-time marketplace notifications.

## Privacy and data disclosure

The app processes email addresses, display names, authentication identifiers, listings, favourites, chat messages and FCM registration tokens. The final Play Console Data Safety form and privacy-policy URL must accurately disclose this Firebase data usage before publication.
